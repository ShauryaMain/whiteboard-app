Privacy

Last updated 1 November 2026

In short

  • We keep what the app needs to work, your sign-in details and your boards, and we count which steps people take in the app so we can see where it’s confusing. No cookies for that.
  • Students can join a class without an account. Their work in a live class isn’t saved to our database, unless they sign in and keep the lesson in a notebook of their own.
  • No ads, no selling your data, no advertising cookies.
  • Want your data or want it deleted? Email us and we’ll do it.

Who we are

Clean Slate is a free online whiteboard for teachers, tutors and their students. If you have a question about your data, email cleanslatesupportoffical@gmail.com.

What we collect

Teachers and anyone who signs in

  • Your sign-in details: your email address. If you sign in with Google, also your name and profile picture, which Google shares when you sign in.
  • Your boards and notebooks: everything you put on them (drawing, text, images, tape, PDFs and pictures you open beside the board or add as pages, and what you write on them, simulations you embed), the page style, a small preview picture of each board, your notebooks’ covers and subjects, and the list of people you’ve shared each one with.
  • Notebook links: if you turn on a notebook’s link, anyone who has the link can read that notebook and download it as a PDF, without an account. If you let the link write too, anyone who opens it and signs in is added to the notebook. You can turn the link off or replace it at any time, and the old link stops working.
  • Fonts you upload: stored so everyone on your boards sees text in that font.
  • Your settings: choices such as your pen colour, shortcuts and graph presets, kept with your account so they follow you to every device.
  • Sharing suggestions: when you share a board by email, Clean Slate can suggest people who already use it, showing their name and email.

Students joining a live class

  • Students join with a class code and a first name. They don’t need an account or an email address.
  • So that only people in the class can see it, the student’s browser gets an invisible sign-in when they join: a random ID with no name or email. We note that this ID joined the class, with the first name they typed, and delete that record about 30 days later.
  • The student’s first name is shared live with the teacher and the class while the class is running.
  • The student’s board is shared live with the teacher. Classmates only see it if the teacher chooses Show to class, which can hide the name. The teacher can also write on it and pin comments.
  • Students’ boards aren’t saved to our database. Before ending the class, the teacher can download everyone’s board as a PDF or pictures (Save boards). That copy goes to the teacher’s own device, not to us.
  • The student’s browser keeps a random ID, the name they typed and a copy of their board for up to a day, so a refresh or rejoining the same class brings their work back.
  • When the class ends, a student can press Keep this lesson. Then a copy of the teacher’s board (as it was at the end of the class) and the student’s own board is kept in their browser for up to three hours while they sign in, and is then saved as a notebook in the student’s own account. That copy belongs to the student, like any notebook.

Feedback you send us

  • Your message, any screenshots you attach, and your email if you tick “It’s OK to email me back”.
  • Device details (browser, screen size and similar) only if “Include device details” is ticked. You can see exactly what’s sent before you send it.

Stored on your own device

  • Your sign-in session, and preferences such as pencil-only mode, the worksheet pane size, where you put the calculator and any clips you keep in view. These stay in your browser.
  • If you try the demo board, a copy of it is kept in your browser, so it’s there when you come back and can be saved to your account when you sign in.
  • Which answers you uncovered under a notebook’s tape, and how you did with Quiz me. These are never sent to us.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads, sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don’t read your boards unless you ask us to help with a problem.

Who helps us run Clean Slate

  • Supabase stores accounts, boards and images, and carries the live sync between devices.
  • Vercel hosts the website and app. We use its visit counts (Vercel Web Analytics) to see which pages get visited and where visitors come from. They don’t use cookies and don’t identify you.
  • PostHog counts the steps people take, from trying the demo board to running a first class, so we can see where Clean Slate is confusing. It uses no cookies: it keeps a random ID in your browser, and for teachers who sign in it uses the account’s ID, never your name or email. Board links and class codes are removed before anything is sent. If the link you followed had a short tag saying where it was posted (like ?ref=reddit), that tag is kept with the steps too. On the demo board only, it may record the screen to show where people get stuck, with anything typed hidden. It never runs on student pages, or on a notebook opened from its link.
  • Google handles Google sign-in, and delivers our emails: sign-in codes, board clean-up reminders and feedback to our support inbox.

Images you add to a board are stored so the board can show them. Anyone who has the direct link to one of those image files can open it, so avoid adding pictures you wouldn’t want shared.

Children and schools

Students, including children, can join a live class without giving us an email address or creating an account. Teachers who use Clean Slate with students are responsible for following their school’s rules on online tools. Parents and schools can contact us at any time about a student’s data.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can delete any board or notebook from your home screen.
  • To keep things tidy, a board that nobody has opened for 30 days is deleted. We email the owner about a week before, with a button to keep the board. Notebooks are never deleted this way: they’re kept until you delete them.
  • To get a copy of your data, or to delete your account and everything in it, email cleanslatesupportoffical@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We’ll reply within 21 days.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
