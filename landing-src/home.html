Why teachers switch to Clean Slate.

Most online whiteboards are built for office meetings. These six things are built for lessons.

Fast in hour three

Drawing happens on your device first, and saving runs quietly in the background. The pen never waits for the internet, even late in a long lesson.

Built for teaching, not meetings.

Most whiteboards are made for office brainstorms. Clean Slate has what a lesson needs, one tap away and clearly labelled.

in every lesson

Pen, highlighter, text

Write, highlight and type in any colour. The eraser only removes ink, so the page stays clean.

Worksheet side by side

Open a PDF or photo beside the board and drag the divider to size it. You can annotate it too.

LinedSquaredDotsIsometric

Paper to suit the task

Blank, lined, squared, dot grid or isometric. The pattern stays put when you zoom.

Copy, paste, move anything

Box-select work to reuse it. Paste a screenshot or drop in a picture and it lands on the board.

when the lesson needs more

Ruler & compass

Construction tools that sit on the board. Rule a straight line, measure a length or draw a perfect circle with your Pencil.

Graphs and grids

Cartesian or polar, any scale, labelled in degrees or radians, with a live preview first.

Simulations on the board

Paste a PhET link (or another interactive) and move or resize it next to your working.

Scientific calculator

A Casio-style calculator with exact answers like ½ and 2√2. Tap Add to board and the answer lands on your page.

new: exact shapes

Shapes, with the angles and lengths marked

Drop an accurate polygon, triangle, quadrilateral, circle or 3D solid straight onto the board. Set the sides, angles and lengths you want, and they’re labelled for you.

  • Sides, interior angle and exterior angle are linked: change one and the others follow.
  • Turn a 3D solid round before you add it, with the hidden edges dashed.
  • It lands as ordinary ink, so you can move it, copy it or rub parts out.

Palm-safe by design

Nothing to tap at the bottom where your hand rests, and the toolbar ignores brushes from your palm mid-stroke.

Pencil-only switches on by itself

The first time you write with a stylus, fingers go back to scrolling and zooming. You can always turn it off.

Runs on the tablet you already own

switching from Microsoft Whiteboard?

Your lessons have somewhere to go.

Microsoft Whiteboard no longer works with personal Microsoft accounts. It’s now only for work and school Microsoft 365 accounts. Clean Slate works with any Google account, and the pen, eraser and menus are where you expect them. See how it compares as a Microsoft Whiteboard alternative.

  1. 1Sign in to Clean Slate with any Google account. It’s free.
  2. 2Bring your old work across. If you exported boards as pictures, drop them straight onto a new board.
  3. 3Share your class code and carry on teaching where you left off.
