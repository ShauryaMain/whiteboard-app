What a teaching whiteboard needs

Most online whiteboards are designed for brainstorming at work: sticky notes, templates and voting. A lesson needs different things, and Clean Slate is built around them.

  • The worksheet beside the board. Open a PDF or a photo in a side pane and teach from it, without switching windows. Write on it, and clip any question onto the board.
  • Tools that say what they do. Every button has a label and a one-line hint, so a new class can use it straight away. When a new tool arrives, a short walkthrough shows you how it works.
  • A pen that keeps up. Drawing happens on your device first and saving runs in the background, so writing never waits on your Wi-Fi.
  • A board for every student. Start a live class and each student gets their own board to work on.

Teach from your worksheet

Choose Insert, then Reference document, and pick a PDF or an image. A long PDF, like a textbook, opens a Choose pages window: tick the pages you need (up to 30) and only those go beside the board. It opens in a pane next to the board. Drag the divider to make it a third, a half or two thirds of the screen.

  • Mark it up like a notebook. Pen, highlighter and eraser in any colour and size, a lasso to move what you wrote, text boxes, and tape that hides an answer until you tap it.
  • Clip a question onto the board. Draw a box round it and it lands on the board, sharp, ready to work on. Keep it in view while you move around the board, or tap Show in document to jump back to it.
  • Lead the class through it. A laser pointer, and Follow me, so everyone's copy scrolls to the page you're on.
  • It's all kept. The document and everything written on it are saved with the board. Find words in it, jump to any page, or add blank, lined or graph pages.

You teach from the question on the left and work it out on the right. Nothing to print, nothing to screenshot.

See every student's working

Press Start live class and Clean Slate gives you a six-character code. Students type it on any device, add their first name, and they're in. They don't need an account or an email address.

Each student sees your board and has a board of their own, with the same tools as yours. Class overview shows every student's board at once, live. Students can put a hand up when they're stuck, and you can open anyone's board to pin a comment or write on their work.

One board for every subject

Pick the paper that suits the lesson: blank, lined, squared, dot grid or isometric, on white or any page colour, chalkboard green included. Text boxes work like a document, with fonts, sizes, colours and highlights, and maths such as x² and ¾ sets itself as you type.

  • Maths: graphs that plot the equations you type, statistics charts, exact shapes with the angles and lengths marked, a ruler, a compass and a scientific calculator with exact answers.
  • Science: label a diagram, or embed a PhET simulation next to your notes.
  • English and humanities: paste in a passage or a source, or open it as a PDF, highlight it and annotate the margins.
  • Languages: lined paper for handwriting, and typed text boxes for vocabulary.

Free, on the devices your school already has

Clean Slate runs in the browser on iPads, Android tablets, Chromebooks, Windows PCs and Macs. There is nothing to install and no paid plan. It's made for a stylus: on an iPad, resting your palm on the screen doesn't leave marks or press buttons.

Common questions

Is Clean Slate free for teachers?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no board limit and no ads. You sign in with Google, or with a code sent to your email, to save and share boards.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The demo board opens straight away with every tool, a ready lesson and a pretend class to try. Your board is kept in your browser, and moves into your account if you sign in.

Do my students need accounts?

No. Students join a live class with a six-character code and their first name.

Can I use it with Zoom, Google Meet or Teams?

Yes. Clean Slate has no video or audio of its own, so run it beside your call. Share your screen, or start a live class so students follow on their own devices.

Which devices does it work on?

Any device with a modern browser: iPads (iPadOS 15 and newer), Android tablets, Chromebooks, Windows, Mac and Linux.
