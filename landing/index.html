new: graphs that plot

Type the equation. Drag the sliders.

Open Maths, then Graph, and type an equation such as y = x² − 3. It’s drawn on the board straight away, beside your working.

  • Letters like a, b and c get sliders, so the class watches the curve change as you drag.
  • Mark roots and turning points, read off a gradient, and shade inequalities or the area under a curve.
  • Bar charts, histograms, scatter graphs with a line of best fit, and box plots from data you type or paste.

exact shapes

Shapes, with the angles and lengths marked

Drop an accurate polygon, triangle, quadrilateral, circle or 3D solid straight onto the board. Set the sides, angles and lengths you want, and they’re labelled for you.

  • Sides, interior angle and exterior angle are linked: change one and the others follow.
  • Turn a 3D solid round before you add it, with the hidden edges dashed.
  • It lands as ordinary ink, so you can move it, copy it or rub parts out.

Questions, answered.

Short, straight answers about what Clean Slate does and doesn’t do.

Is Clean Slate really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no board limit and no ads.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The demo board opens with no sign-up, no login and nothing to download, with a ready lesson and a pretend class to try. Your board is kept in your browser, and moves into your account when you sign in with Google or an email address.

Do my students need accounts?

No. Students join a live class with a six-character code and their first name.

Does it work on an iPad with Apple Pencil?

Yes. It runs in Safari on iPadOS 15 and newer. Once you write with a Pencil, only the Pencil draws, and resting your palm on the screen leaves no marks. See the whiteboard for iPad.

Can I open a PDF on the whiteboard?

Yes. Open a PDF of up to 30 pages, or an image, in a pane beside the board, and write on it.

Is it a replacement for Microsoft Whiteboard or Jamboard?

Yes. Microsoft Whiteboard no longer works with personal accounts and Google shut Jamboard down in 2024. Clean Slate works in any browser, and you sign in with Google or any email address. See the Microsoft Whiteboard alternative and the Jamboard alternative.

Can it plot graphs?

Yes. Type an equation and it is drawn on the board, with sliders for letters like a, b and c. It also draws bar charts, histograms, scatter graphs and box plots. See the whiteboard for math.

Can it draw accurate shapes for maths?

Yes. Insert, then Shape draws polygons, triangles, quadrilaterals, circles and 3D solids to the sides and angles you set, with the measurements labelled. See the whiteboard for math.

Does it have video or audio calls?

No. Use it beside Zoom, Google Meet or Teams.

Are my boards saved?

Yes. When you're signed in, boards save automatically. A board that nobody opens for 30 days is deleted, and you get an email first with a button to keep it.

Which devices and browsers does it work on?

Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on iPads, Android tablets, Chromebooks, Windows, Mac and Linux. There is nothing to install.
