Why teachers switch to Clean Slate.

It started as a fix for one tutor’s lagging whiteboard. These are the six things that made her class stay.

Fast in hour three

Drawing happens on your device first, and saving runs quietly in the background. The pen never waits for the internet, even late in a long lesson.

built after Microsoft Whiteboard kept slowing down mid-lesson

Every student gets a board

Students join with a 6-character code and no account. They follow your board, work on their own, and you can look in on anyone and pin a comment.

free, with no 2-hour session limit

The worksheet beside the board

Open a PDF or photo in a side pane, size it how you like and write on it. Nothing to print and nothing to screenshot.

no work or school account needed

Real teaching tools

A ruler and compass, graph and grid paper, simulations, and a scientific calculator that gives exact answers.

new: a Casio-style calculator

Made for the Pencil

Rest your hand on the screen without stray marks or switched tools. Pencil-only mode turns on by itself the first time you write.

works on iPads back to 2014

Free, with no catch

No board limit, no ads, no card and nothing to install. Try the demo board without even signing in.

Palm-safe by design

Nothing to tap at the bottom where your hand rests, and the toolbar ignores brushes from your palm mid-stroke.

Pencil-only switches on by itself

The first time you write with a stylus, fingers go back to scrolling and zooming. You can always turn it off.

Runs on the tablet you already own

Works on iPads going back to 2014, on Samsung and other Android tablets, and on laptops. Nothing to install.

switching from Microsoft Whiteboard?

Your lessons have somewhere to go.

Microsoft Whiteboard no longer works with personal Microsoft accounts. It’s now only for work and school Microsoft 365 accounts. Clean Slate was built to replace it for a working tutor, so the pen, eraser and menus are where you expect them.

  1. 1Sign in to Clean Slate with any Google account. It’s free.
  2. 2Bring your old work across. If you exported boards as pictures, drop them straight onto a new board.
  3. 3Share your class code and carry on teaching where you left off.
