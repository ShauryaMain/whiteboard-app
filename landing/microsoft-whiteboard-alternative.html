sign in with Google or any email A free Microsoft Whiteboard alternative

Microsoft Whiteboard no longer works with personal Microsoft accounts. Clean Slate is a free online whiteboard that runs in any browser, signs in with Google or any email address, and has the pen, eraser and menus where you expect them.

Try it now, no sign-in Start free

The demo opens a real board in your browser. Nothing to install, and your board is kept in your browser until you sign in.

What happened to Microsoft Whiteboard

Microsoft has ended Whiteboard for personal Microsoft accounts, and its standalone Windows, iPad and Android apps retire on 16 October 2026. Whiteboard continues only for work and school Microsoft 365 accounts, inside the web version and Teams.

Source: Microsoft Support.

What will feel familiar

  • A pen, a highlighter and an eraser, with colours and sizes.
  • Text boxes and pictures you can move around.
  • A ruler for straight lines.
  • Keyboard shortcuts, which you can change.
  • A board you can pan and zoom, with undo and redo.
  • Sharing, so several people write on one board at once.

Clean Slate also adds tools aimed at teaching: graphs that plot the equations you type, shapes drawn to the angles and lengths you set, a compass, a scientific calculator, and lined, squared, dot grid or isometric paper.

Moving your boards across

  1. Sign in to Clean Slate with Google, or with a code sent to any email address.
  2. Bring your old work across. If you exported boards from Microsoft Whiteboard as pictures, drop them onto a new board.
  3. Carry on. Draw over the picture, or start fresh beside it.

Clean Slate can't open Microsoft Whiteboard files directly.

Common questions

Is Clean Slate free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no board limit and no ads.

Does it work on Mac, Android and Linux?

Yes. It runs in the browser, so it works on Mac, Windows, Linux, Chromebooks, iPads and Android tablets.

Can I import my Microsoft Whiteboard boards?

Not directly. Export them from Microsoft Whiteboard as pictures and drop the pictures onto a Clean Slate board.

Does it work offline?

No. Clean Slate needs an internet connection.

Is it open source?

No. It is free to use, but the code is not open source.

Do I need to sign up to try it?

No. The demo board opens with no sign-up.
