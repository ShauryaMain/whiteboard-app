free notebooks · any device A free notes app for students

Clean Slate notebooks are paper pages you write on with a pen, a finger or a mouse, in the browser on a Chromebook, a Windows laptop, an iPad or an Android tablet. Keep as many as you like, sorted into subjects, and cover the answers with tape to test yourself. It's free.

Notebooks that feel like paper

A notebook is a stack of real pages, A4 or US Letter, that you scroll through. Ink stays on the page you start it on, the way it does on paper.

  • Pick the paper for each page: lined, squared, dot grid, blank, Cornell notes, maths paper with a margin, or isometric dots.
  • All your pages at a glance. Jump to any page, move pages up or down, copy one, turn it sideways, or add a page anywhere.
  • Hand it in as a PDF. Export the whole notebook, one page or a few, each on a real A4 or Letter page, ready to print or upload.
  • Write over your slides. Add pages from a PDF, like lecture slides or a worksheet, and write straight over them.

Revise with tape and Quiz me

Drag tape over an answer, a definition or a key word, and it stays hidden until you tap it. Tap again to cover it back up.

  • Quiz me covers every piece of tape on a page and asks them one at a time. Say the answer, check it, and say whether you knew it.
  • Try the ones you missed straight away, or start again. How you did last time is kept on your device.
  • Printing a test for yourself? Export with the tape on, and the answers stay covered on paper.

It works on flashcard-style notes, Cornell notes, vocabulary lists and worked examples alike.

Maths and science, built in

A notebook has every tool of the Clean Slate whiteboard on every page: graphs that plot the equations you type, statistics charts, exact shapes with the angles and lengths marked, a ruler, a compass and a scientific calculator. Type x^2 or 3/4 in a text box and it turns into real maths. Hold the pen still at the end of a line and it straightens.

Open your textbook as a PDF beside your notes, and clip a diagram or a question from it onto the page.

Tidy, and kept for good

Your notebooks sit on a shelf on your home screen, most recently used first. Give each one a cover colour and a sticker, and sort them into subjects like Maths, Physics or English. Search finds them by name.

Notebooks are never deleted for being left alone, so last term's notes are still there at exam time.

Share notes with your class

Share a notebook by email, or with a link. Choose who the link works for:

  • Anyone with the link can look. They don't need an account. They can read it, peek under the tape, quiz themselves and download it as a PDF.
  • Anyone with the link can write on it too. They sign in first, then you write on it together, live.

Turn the link off, or swap it for a new one, whenever you like.

Keep every lesson

If your teacher or tutor runs live classes on Clean Slate, press Keep this lesson when the class ends. Their board and your own working go into a notebook of your own, in a subject called Lessons, ready to revise from.

On the devices students actually have

Clean Slate runs in the browser on Chromebooks, Windows laptops, Macs, iPads and Android tablets, and the same notebook is on all of them. On an iPad it's made for Apple Pencil: rest your palm on the screen and it leaves no marks. A shared notebook opens on a phone too, for reading and quizzing on the bus.

It's free, with no limit on notebooks and no ads. It does need an internet connection.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no limit on how many notebooks you make, and no ads. You sign in with Google, or with a code sent to any email address.

Does it work on a Chromebook, Windows laptop or Android tablet?

Yes. It runs in the browser, so it works on Chromebooks, Windows, Mac, Linux, iPads and Android tablets, with nothing to install.

Can my teacher send me homework in it?

Yes. If your teacher gives you a class code, join with it and you get a notebook for that class. Homework arrives in it with its due date: write on the pages, press Hand in, and see your teacher's marks and comments when it comes back. The notebook stays yours.

Can I write with Apple Pencil?

Yes. On an iPad (iPadOS 15 and newer), once you write with the Pencil only the Pencil draws, and your palm leaves no marks. See Clean Slate on iPad.

Can I import a PDF and write on it?

Yes. Add pages from a PDF (pick up to 30 at a time) and write straight over them. A notebook can have up to 500 pages.

Can I export my notes?

Yes. Export the whole notebook, one page or a range of pages as a single PDF.

Is it an alternative to Goodnotes or Notability?

For many students, yes: Clean Slate notebooks are free and run in any browser, including on Chromebooks and Windows laptops. It doesn't search handwriting, record audio or work offline.

Who can see my notebooks?

Only you, until you share one by email or turn on its link. You can turn a link off or replace it at any time.

Will my notes be deleted if I don't open them?

No. Notebooks are kept until you delete them.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The demo notebook opens with no sign-up: a page of revision notes with the answers under tape. It is kept in your browser, and moves into your account if you sign in.
