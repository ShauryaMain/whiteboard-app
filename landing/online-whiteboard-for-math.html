ruler · compass · graph paper · calculator A free online whiteboard for math

Clean Slate is an online whiteboard with real math tools built in: a ruler and compass, graph paper you can set up your way, and a scientific calculator that gives exact answers. It's free, and you can try it without signing up.

Try it now, no sign-in Start free with Google

The demo opens a real board in your browser. Nothing to install, nothing saved.

Geometry tools: ruler and compass

Open the Maths menu and pick Ruler or Compass. Both sit on the board like the real things.

  • Ruler: draw perfectly straight lines at any angle and measure lengths.
  • Compass: drag the pin to set the centre and the pencil to set the radius, then draw circles and arcs.

Graph paper, set up your way

The Graph tool places a set of axes on the board. You choose:

  • Cartesian or polar axes.
  • The cell size, and how many columns and rows.
  • The value of each cell, with a unit such as cm or m.
  • Trig labels, so the axis is marked in multiples of π.
  • Whether to show the axes, the numbers and a unit circle.

A live preview shows the graph before it goes on the board. Then drag it into place and draw on it.

A scientific calculator with exact answers

The calculator is laid out like a school scientific calculator. It gives exact answers such as ½, 2√2 or π/4, and one tap switches between the exact form and a decimal. It works in degrees or radians, keeps a history of your last answers, and its Add to board button writes the calculation onto the board.

Squared, dot grid and isometric paper

Set the whole page to squared paper for graphs and tables, dot grid for diagrams, or isometric paper for 3D shapes. The pattern stays in place as you zoom and pan, and the eraser never rubs it out.

Write maths by hand

Maths is quicker to write than to type. Clean Slate is made for a stylus: the pen follows your hand, a scribble over a mistake rubs it out, and on an iPad your palm can rest on the screen. Typed text boxes are there when you want them.

Common questions

Does it have a ruler and a compass?

Yes. Both are in the Maths menu. The ruler draws straight lines and measures lengths, and the compass draws circles and arcs.

Does it have graph paper?

Yes, in two ways. The Page menu sets the whole board to squared paper, and the Graph tool places Cartesian or polar axes with the scale you choose.

Can it plot a function for me?

No. The Graph tool gives you labelled axes and you draw the curve yourself, as you would on paper.

Is the calculator a graphing calculator?

No. It is a scientific calculator with exact answers, fractions, powers, roots, logs and trig in degrees or radians.

Is it free?

Yes. You can also try the demo board without signing up.
