free for tutors A free online whiteboard for tutoring

Clean Slate is an online whiteboard for one-to-one and small-group tutoring. Keep the past paper beside the board, write as you would on paper, and pick up next week exactly where you stopped.

Try a tutoring board, no sign-in Start free

The demo opens a real board in your browser. Nothing to install, and your board is kept in your browser until you sign in.

Built around a tutoring session

  • The question stays in view. Open the worksheet or past paper as a PDF or photo beside the board, and write on it too. Clip the question you're on onto the board, or keep it floating in view.
  • It feels like writing. Pen, highlighter and eraser are made for a stylus. Scribble over something to rub it out.
  • Your student can write as well. Share a board by email and you both write on it at the same time.
  • Next week is one tap away. Every board saves automatically and shows a preview picture, so last lesson is easy to find.

Past papers, clipped onto the board

Open a whole past paper or textbook and pick just the pages you need. Highlight the key words, cover the answers with tape until your student has had a go, and draw a box round a question to put it on the board beside your working.

Everything you write on the paper is saved with the board, so next week it's all where you left it.

Long lessons stay quick

What you draw appears on your own device first, and saving happens quietly in the background. The pen doesn't wait for the internet, so writing feels the same at the end of a two-hour session as it did at the start.

The tools a maths or science tutor reaches for

The Maths menu has a ruler, a compass, a Graph tool and a scientific calculator that gives exact answers such as ½ or 2√2. Type an equation into the Graph tool and it's plotted on the board, with sliders for letters like a, b and c. You can send a calculator answer straight to the board.

Need a diagram for the question? Insert, then Shape draws a polygon, triangle, quadrilateral, circle or 3D solid to the sides and angles you type, with the measurements labelled.

For other subjects, switch the page to lined paper, paste in a text to annotate, or embed a PhET simulation.

Use it alongside Zoom, Meet or Teams

Clean Slate is a whiteboard, not a video call. Keep your usual call running for voice and video and use Clean Slate for the working. Share your screen, or send your student the board so they have it on their own device.

Small groups: a board for each student

For a group, start a live class. Students join with a six-character code and no account. Each one gets their own board with the full set of tools. See all their boards at once, open any of them while they work, and pin a comment or write a correction where it matters.

Common questions

What is the best free online whiteboard for math tutoring?

That depends on what you need. Clean Slate is free and has graphs that plot equations, exact labelled shapes, a ruler, a compass, a scientific calculator with exact answers, and a pane for the worksheet beside the board. It has no built-in video call.

Does it work with Zoom?

Yes. Run Clean Slate in your browser beside Zoom, Google Meet or Teams, and either share your screen or share the board with your student.

Does my student need an account?

Not for a live class: they join with a code and their first name. To write on the same board as you, they sign in free with Google or with a code sent to their email.

Can I keep a separate board for each student?

Yes. Make as many boards as you like and name them. Each one saves automatically. A board that nobody opens for 30 days is deleted, and you get an email first with a button to keep it.

Can I write on a past paper?

Yes. Open it as a PDF beside the board (pick up to 30 pages from a long one), then write, highlight, type and cover answers with tape. Clip any question onto the board. It's all saved with the board.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. There is no paid plan and no board limit.
