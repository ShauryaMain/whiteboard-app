no account · no login · no download A free online whiteboard with no sign-up

Open the board and start drawing. There is no account to create, no login and nothing to download. Sign in only if you want to keep your boards on every device or teach a live class.

Open the whiteboard Start free

The demo opens a real board in your browser. Nothing to install, and your board is kept in your browser until you sign in.

What you get without an account

The no-sign-up board is the real Clean Slate whiteboard, running in your browser tab. Start from a ready lesson, a tutoring graph or a quick tour, or just draw.

  • Pen, highlighter, eraser, text boxes, and scribble to erase.
  • Blank, lined, squared, dot grid and isometric paper.
  • Graphs that plot equations, exact shapes, a ruler, a compass and a scientific calculator.
  • Pictures you paste or drop in, and a PDF or image beside the board to mark up and clip onto the board.
  • Text boxes with fonts, colours and typed maths, and any page colour.
  • Select, move, copy, paste, undo and redo.
  • A pretend live class, with five students to look in on.

Your board stays in your own browser, so it's there when you come back. It isn't sent to us unless you sign in, and then it's saved to your account.

When it's worth signing in

Signing in is free. Use a Google account, or have a code sent to your email. It adds the things that need somewhere to keep your work:

  • Boards that save automatically, with a preview of each one. The board you made in the demo comes with you.
  • Sharing a board so someone else can write on it with you.
  • Live classes, where every student gets their own board.

Students never need an account

If you teach, your students join a live class with a six-character code and their first name. They don't sign up for anything.

No download, on any device

Clean Slate is a web page, not an app to install. It works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on iPads, Android tablets, Chromebooks, Windows, Mac and Linux.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and no ads.

Is anything saved if I don't sign in?

Only in your own browser. The demo keeps your board there, so it's waiting when you come back, and it's saved to your account if you sign in. Browsers can clear this, for example in a private window or after a week or so away in Safari.

Can other people join my board without signing in?

Students can join a live class with a code and no account. The teacher who starts the class signs in with Google or an email address.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in the browser.
